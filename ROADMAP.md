# Research Roadmap

## Phase 0 — Research Foundation
Map Toumai/telesurgery architecture, surgical CV, datasets, distribution shift, reliable inference, and networking. Produce an evidence matrix and revised research questions.

## Phase 1 — Gap Validation
Stress-test candidate contributions against primary literature. Define precise hypotheses, tasks, comparison methods, and exclusion criteria.

## Phase 2 — Dataset & Task Feasibility
Acquire permitted datasets, verify licences, establish preprocessing, define evaluation splits, and reproduce at least one accepted baseline.

## Phase 3 — Baseline Experiments
Establish clean-distribution performance and reliability metrics with versioned configurations.

## Phase 4 — Shift Benchmarking
Introduce controlled visual and network-induced degradations. Evaluate individual and compound shifts across severity levels.

## Phase 5 — Reliability Methods
Evaluate justified calibration, uncertainty, OOD, selective-prediction, and/or conformal approaches.

## Phase 6 — Network-Aware Reliability
Where supported by earlier evidence, combine visual information with network/system telemetry and compare against image-only reliability estimation.

## Phase 7 — Validation & Publication
Replicate key results, run ablations and sensitivity analyses, audit claims, document limitations, freeze environments/configurations, and prepare research artefacts.
