## Core findings
1. **The literatures are nearly disjoint.** No retained telesurgery source evaluates an AI model. Only two surgical-CV evaluations touch networks: Endo-C6's clip-level abstraction and a delivery-latency study.
2. **New overlap since P0.6:** VISTA (May 2026) is a surgical forward-video network-impairment benchmark (H.264/UDP/NetEm Gilbert–Elliott, five tiers, PSNR/SSIM/VMAF/freeze) with human outcomes and no AI. It occupies the pipeline layer P0.6 called "Level B". NetFI adds trace-informed network fault injection. A MICCAI 2026 paper adds phase temporal-stability reliability (TFI).
3. **The network→DNN-accuracy relationship is established outside medicine** (DDS, AccMPEG, DNN-aware retransmission, VANET). Telemetry-based QoS forecasting is established in teleoperated driving.
4. **Occupancy:** of 26 cells, 3 SATURATED, 11 OCCUPIED, 4 PARTIAL, 8 NOT FOUND IN RETAINED EVIDENCE.
5. **Track A** survives only as a mechanism question (matched video quality, different mechanism) and is **absorbed** as the shift substrate.
6. **Track B** survives only as an empirical reliability audit of phase recognition under decoder-mediated and natural shift, plus telemetry-stratified Mondrian calibration. It is **narrowed** to the reliability layer.
7. **Track C** survives as the **primary** question. It asks whether receiver-observable network and transport/decoder telemetry improves failure detection and selective deferral **beyond a strong video-only monitor** in online phase recognition. **Residual risk is high** because generic networking venues were only shallowly searched.
8. **Decision:** merge into one integrated study (RQ1 primary; RQ2, RQ3 secondary), anchored on online phase recognition with Cholec80 (development), PhaKIR (external, descriptive) and optionally AutoLaparo.
9. **Claim audit:** no affirmative novelty/"first" claim exists in programme text. Prohibited claims and permitted framing are now recorded.
