# Hypotheses

**H1a.** V0+V1+N1+N2 improves frame-level failure-detection AUROC over V0+V1 on held-out procedures (procedure-level 95% BCa bootstrap CI excluding 0); 0.02 is the smallest effect of interest (pre-registration v1.1 §7.1).

**H1b.** The same comparison reduces AURC of selective deferral (CI excluding 0).

**H1c (conditional).** Telemetry yields a longer median alarm lead time for sustained failure events at a fixed false-alarm rate.

**H1d (conditional).** The gain persists under leave-one-impairment-tier-out evaluation.

**Rejection.** H1a and H1b both fail → reported as a primary negative result.
